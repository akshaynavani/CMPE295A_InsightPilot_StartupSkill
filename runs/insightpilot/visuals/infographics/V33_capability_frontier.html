Why this chart argues for the design

The bottom bar is the whole thesis

If multi-step reasoning were reliable, correctness could stay pre-encoded and nobody would need a trace. Because it is not, the trace is what makes the steps usable. The weakness is the reason for the architecture rather than an objection to it.

A caution on every bar

Benchmark scores are not deployment accuracy

Annotation errors in the standard text-to-SQL benchmarks are documented well enough that deltas of a few points carry no meaning. Treat each figure as an ordering signal rather than a specification.

What is genuinely favourable

Tool access standardised, on the record

MCP shipped November 2024 with a Postgres reference server and was adopted by four major vendors within thirteen months, then handed to a neutral steward. That is what makes a pluggable connector layer tractable for a small team.
