Why the crowded cell is crowded

Pre-encoding buys correctness by narrowing scope

Thirty tables per Genie Agent. A hand-authored 32K-token Cortex model. Copilot cannot compute a metric that is not already a measure. These are not bugs, they are the reliability mechanism, and the better it works the fewer questions get attempted.

Why the open cell is open

Multi-step reasoning measures about 15%

On DABstep's hard split, drawn from a real financial analytics workload, leading agents score roughly 15% against 76% on the easy split. The space is empty for a reason, and a positioning that claims an empty quadrant without saying why is claiming everyone else is careless.

The inversion

The axes are not independent

If multi-step execution were reliable, correctness could stay pre-encoded and nobody would need a trace. Because it is not, the trace is what makes the steps usable at all. Occupying the far right requires occupying the top.
