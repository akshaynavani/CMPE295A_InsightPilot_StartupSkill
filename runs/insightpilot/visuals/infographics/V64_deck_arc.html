InsightPilot · Pitch deck

Fourteen slides, every title a claim rather than a category, and three of them concede

The arc is fixed: cold-open on the problem, demolish the status quo, then show the mechanism. Teardown before build, because credibility is earned by demonstrating you know why everything else falls short. Read the titles in sequence and they carry the argument on their own.

teardown · a third of the time the hinge mechanism where a normal deck inflates and this one concedes
Slide 1

People stop asking questions when the answer takes a month

One to four weeks for an ad-hoc question, so requesters route around it. 76% have decided without data. The market leader is doing nothing, and it is free and instant

visual · one message, answered four days later
Slide 2

Every vendor tried a chat box, and it fails in a documented way

81.2% of analysed failures are schema-level, and failure looks like a plausible but incorrect answer. One such feature shipped inside the most widely deployed BI tool on earth and still retired

visual · the graveyard, with cause of death
Slide 3

The problem is not slow answers, it is that fast answers cannot be trusted

Trust in data went from 66% to 83% in a year, and 59% are funding semantic layers to constrain output. The budget is already moving toward verifiability, to a different answer than ours

visual · latency evidence beside trust evidence
Slide 4

Incumbents buy correctness by refusing the questions nobody anticipated

A thirty-table cap, a hand-authored token-capped model, no metric that is not already a measure. Not bugs. The reliability mechanism

visual · each bound, in its own documentation
Slide 5 · the hinge

Stop promising correctness, start making failure cheap to catch

About 15% on hard tasks, and consistency degrades faster than capability. If multi-hop execution were reliable nobody would need a trace. Because it is not, the trace is what makes the hops usable

visual · the two-axis map, open quadrant empty
Slide 6

The unit is the investigation, and it ships with its own audit trail

A plan shown before a single query runs, four agents across the real sources, lineage per handoff, each hop re-runnable. Verification consumes zero model tokens

visual · the investigation pipeline
Slide 7

In the demo, the system catches its own join failure in 40 seconds

Step three returns amber: the join preserved 13,061 of 14,201 rows, and the file has four region codes against the warehouse's six. That takes twenty minutes to find by hand

visual · the amber hop as the analyst sees it
Slide 8

Verification by decomposition, corroborated by the strongest incumbent

Cheap SQL checks, never the model checking itself. Reflection and self-critique declined on principle. The intellectual ancestor is financial audit: the workpaper, not the number, is the deliverable

visual · the verify and replan loop
Slide 9 · concession

The honest arithmetic is about 2.9 times, and on familiar single-source questions it is 1.0

Ninety minutes to thirty-one, band 1.8 to 4.2. Cross-source reconciliation is the big one. On a familiar single-source question the analyst should keep writing SQL, and we say so

visual · six frictions, four attacked
Slide 10 · concession

The addressable market is modest, built bottom up, and we are not calling it bigger

About 1.24 million analysts globally, total $1.1B and serviceable $123M, pessimistic corner $38M. A share-of-a-huge-category slide would contradict our own market-type analysis

visual · the bottom-up build with its corners
Slide 11

At this contract value only zero-cost channels survive, so we ship open source

Outbound would need a four-to-nine-seat account just to repay acquisition. The loop closes when an exported artifact is shareable and checkable. Adoption precedes revenue structurally

visual · the loop, with the export as its edge
Slide 12

This does not become a warehouse feature, because the value is in spanning

One company raised about $128.7M attacking the highest-value part of this loop and became a division. The residual, stated: if a real customer's questions live in one warehouse, the argument is true and worthless

visual · the petal, and which budget each customer comes from
Slide 13 · concession

Our evidence is thin, and the one number that matters is untested

No customers, no revenue, no accuracy measurement. The beachhead's objection is correct as stated. One afternoon, five analysts and a timed task settles it, and it precedes further building

visual · three untested claims, ranked by blast radius
Slide 14

What we are asking for is ten analysts and one heterogeneous stack

Not funding. Ten analysts for an hour each, one stack with a warehouse and an operational database and a file feed, and advisor challenge on the two research risks

visual · the two-semester timeline, harness first
On the arc
