Rule one

One adaptive system, no tiers

Same screens for every edge. Only defaults change. A business edition would be a second product to maintain and a second place for the two to drift apart.

Rule two

Never present confidence as accuracy

No percentage scores. The system reports what it checked and what it is unsure about, which are different claims and must not be collapsed into one number.

Rule three

Doubt outranks reassurance

An amber result beats a green one in weight, colour and position. A product whose failures are quiet is the exact failure mode this design exists to prevent.
