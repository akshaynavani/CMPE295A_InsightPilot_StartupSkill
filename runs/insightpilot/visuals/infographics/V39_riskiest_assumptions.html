The sequencing this implies

Verification cost before anything else

It gates the two engineering risks and the architecture itself. If checking a trace costs what re-deriving the answer costs, a more accurate planner does not help anyone.

Two rows already lean negative

That is a reason to measure, not to stop

The benchmark evidence on multi-step reasoning points the wrong way, and the review-burden risk was modelled rather than discovered. Both are why the first semester leads with measurement and not agents.

One row is a refusal

The moat candidate sits here to stay testable

It is listed so the instrumentation exists from day one, not so it can be counted as evidence now. Confirming it would take ninety days of real use, and until then it is not claimed anywhere.
