What he does today Messages an analyst on Thursday. Gets an answer Monday, if he is lucky. Otherwise builds the slide from memory and hedges the language. He is part of the 76% of businesses that have decided without consulting data because it was too hard to get.
What changes Ninety seconds of his attention, then a two-hour wait for six minutes of hers. The number reaches his slide with an analyst's name beside it, which is what makes it usable in a room where he will be challenged.
Why the gate is dignity

It replaces doing the work with checking it

He was already blocked, by three days rather than two hours. The gate does not stop him getting an answer. It changes what he is waiting for.

What he still cannot do

Nothing here makes him self-sufficient

He cannot tell a good answer from a plausible wrong one, and no design changes that. The system makes his dependence fast and legible rather than removing it. On a Friday before a holiday, the gate is a wall.
