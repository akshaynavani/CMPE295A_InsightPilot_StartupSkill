InsightPilot · High edge

The largest measured gain in the pack, from one mechanism used alone

Dr. Chen reviews other people's analyses. His usual cost is two hours, and the worst outcome is that the work turns out fine, because then he has spent two hours confirming something with no way to spend less next time. He touches no agent, no orchestrator and no connector. He reads and challenges.

0:00

Opens the export in a text editor first

To confirm it is genuinely self-contained. It is. That act is what earns the next fifteen minutes.

0:02

Reads the plan, not the answer

Seven steps: three fetch, two analytics, one model, one render.

0:04

Goes straight to the join he did not write

Re-runs that single step against current data. Ninety seconds, against twenty minutes rebuilding it.

0:07

Checks the model card for leakage

Target definition in full, entity-grouped split with a thirty-day embargo, and last-login excluded as a leakage candidate with the reason stated. It caught the thing he would have caught.

0:12

Catches what the system could not

Support-ticket counts rank third. He knows tickets migrated systems in April, so pre-April counts are structurally lower. The data is wrong in a way no schema records.

0:19

Overrides, annotates, signs off

Marked as a data-provenance issue rather than a model error. Top two drivers hold.

What makes it work

Renunciation is the credibility

The model card publishes what it did not have time to try. An exhaustive not-attempted list is what makes the rest believable to the person who has to approve it.

The honest ceiling

April beat the system, and always will

No amount of lineage, verification or model capability catches a ticketing migration that lives only in his head. What the design can do is make the correction cheap, attributed and durable.

What he refuses

A screening tool, not a forecast

He will not accept a four-minute time-boxed baseline for a production decision, and says so in his note. That is exactly what the product should want him to say.

Two hours becomes nineteen minutes. The largest ratio in the pack, and it belongs to the persona who is not the beachhead.
