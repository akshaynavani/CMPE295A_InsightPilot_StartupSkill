The two that win most often are free, and neither is a product

A competitor is anything that consumes the budget or the hour. That makes doing nothing and writing the SQL yourself the market leaders, and the failure column is where the shipped products genuinely diverge.

Failure mode one

Pre-encoded correctness cannot answer the unanticipated

Every incumbent that gets correctness right requires an artifact authored before the question, and that artifact is bounded. The better the mechanism, the narrower the set of questions attempted.

Failure mode two

Plausible-but-wrong is undetectable at delivery

Every incumbent's answer is to show the SQL, which hands verification back to the analyst. Reading a query you did not write is not obviously cheaper than writing it.

What cannot be claimed

No head-to-head accuracy comparison exists

Not one commercial agentic-BI product publishes an execution-accuracy figure against any public benchmark. Every comparison here is mechanism-level, and the pack forbids implying a measured advantage.
