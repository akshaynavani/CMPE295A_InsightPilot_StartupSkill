Why component 2 is high risk

The field measures this at about 15%

DABstep draws 450 or more tasks from a real financial analytics workload, classes 84% of them hard, and reports leading agents at roughly 15% on that split against 76% on the easy one. Typed schemas, static validation and short plans are dampeners against a capability limit, not solutions to it.

Why component 4 is the soft number

The checks catch countable failures, provably

Their coverage of the plausible failure class is unmeasured, which is why the whitepaper discounts this mechanism from an observed 6.7x to a claimed 4.0x on judgement alone. The seeded-error set replaces the judgement with a measurement.
