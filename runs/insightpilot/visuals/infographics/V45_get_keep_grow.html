Why this is unusually strong in principle. A dashboard is shareable and not checkable. A chat answer is neither. An investigation with its lineage is both, which makes forwarding it a professional act rather than a risk, so improving the differentiator improves distribution at the same time.
Which makes the export a growth requirement disguised as a product feature. It is the highest-leverage build decision in the funnel, cheap now and expensive to retrofit onto a stateful interface.
The concession this funnel has to make

The best growth vector is the one that could break retention

Stakeholder growth increases analyst review load. The tension is unresolved, and it gets measured before it gets designed around.

What the funnel does not have

No paid acquisition, no conversion rate, no virality

Paid acquisition is ruled out by arithmetic rather than preference. Monetisation is out of scope, so the funnel stops at a buyer being asked. The loop runs inside one organisation, because the artifact contains that customer's data.
