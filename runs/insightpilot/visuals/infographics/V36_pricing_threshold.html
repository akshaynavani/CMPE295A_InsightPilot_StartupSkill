Three tiers, deliberately simple
Open coreFree

The entire differentiator ships free: plan surface, per-hop verification, hop-level lineage, single-hop re-run, export, audit log. Charging for the thing that recruits the next analyst would break the only channel whose economics work.

Team$75 per analyst/mo

Semantic-layer consumption, shared review queue, history search, binding review, reconciliation reporting. What a team needs and an individual does not, which is also what the buyer evaluates.

EnterpriseNegotiated

Managed deployment, support commitments, single sign-on, retention controls. Not a growth vector.

What would falsify this
Multi-source share below 15%. Delivered hours fall to about 23 and the threshold fails at three attempts or more
Expected attempts above five. Margin approaches 1.0 and the customer pays to break even
Verification cost ratio above 0.8. The 59-minute saving is fiction and the whole computation is void
Analyst cost of $80k rather than $120k. Break-even hours rise by half and margin at three attempts falls to about 1.1
The right meter and the right invoice differ

Price the investigation, bill the seat

The market already meters the question: one vendor bills per message on success, another caps its agent at 25 queries a month. Tokens are wrong because the customer already pays them. Seats are wrong because value is lumpy, and are recommended anyway because a simple invoice costs no sales team.

Who the conversation is with

The payer has never used the product

The analyst advocates but holds no budget, and her stated willingness to pay is not a useful signal. The platform owner can only say no. That is why the paid tier holds the artifacts the budget holder evaluates rather than the features the user enjoys.

One section left deliberately empty

No pricing-power argument

The usual claim is that price rises as a data moat compounds. The candidate here is unevidenced and confirming it needs 90 days of real deployment. Until then the section stays blank, and anything downstream asserting pricing power is asserting a moat this pack declines to claim.
