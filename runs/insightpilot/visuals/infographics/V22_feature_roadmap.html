The evaluation harness is feature one. Three of four agents are not in scope

Ordered by leverage on the riskiest claim rather than by visibility. That produces one counter-intuitive result, stated up front so it is not mistaken for an error: the first month of a build project contains almost no building, because a demo with four agents and no measurement cannot tell anyone whether it works.

Decided in advance, not in week ten

The cut order, agreed before the pressure arrives

1Visualization agent. A table is an acceptable answer. Most demo-visible, least evidence-bearing.
2REST connector. Postgres plus CSV already demonstrates heterogeneity.
3ML agent. Largest single build, and A2 already establishes it is not the differentiator.
4Override re-flow. Degrade to invalidate-and-replan, the honest fallback anyway.
The line to hold

Items 1 to 15, or a demo with no evidence

Ship those and the project has a measurable, verifiable, exportable investigation across two source types plus a published accuracy number. Ship four agents, charts and no harness and it has a demo, with every claim in the pack still untested. A cut decided in advance is a scope decision. The same cut in week ten is a failure, and the two are indistinguishable in a final report.
