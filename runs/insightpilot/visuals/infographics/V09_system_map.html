Everything runs inside the customer's boundary, with one arrow leaving

Orchestration, agents, connectors, bindings, lineage and the audit log all sit inside the customer environment. The single crossing is to a model provider, on the customer's own key, and it is configurable down to schema only. That is what makes neutrality a property rather than a claim.

What is absent by design

No write path exists

Not disabled, absent. Combined with a read-only role, the blast radius of a total compromise is what the analyst could already read.

Single tenant by construction

No shared control plane

There is no cross-organisation path to secure, because there is none. Growth is a distribution problem rather than an infrastructure one.

What it costs

The flywheel is the customer's

Each deployment's binding store starts empty. A hundred deployments produce a hundred learning curves, which directly caps the moat candidate.
