InsightPilot · Sales roadmap

The person who feels the pain has no budget, and the person with budget never uses it

That gap is the sale. The only viable channel produces users rather than revenue, so every dollar arrives through internal expansion, which is a sales process whether or not anyone is called a salesperson.

Organisation map
CFO and COObudget authority above about $50k
VP of data and analyticseconomic buyer · signs · does not use
Analytics managerruns the queue, owns the team's time
Data platform leadsecurity veto, no budget
Staff data scientisttechnical credibility
Data analysts, five to fifteenuser and champion · installs · advocates · cannot buy
Business stakeholders sit outside this tree entirely. They generate the demand and have no say in the purchase
Two structural facts. The gap between who feels the pain and who holds the budget is the sale. And the platform lead can end the process without being in it, because a security veto needs no budget and no meeting.

The contestable claim this map makes: the analyst outranks the budget owner. In a self-serve motion the product is already in use before any purchase conversation exists, so the VP is not deciding whether to try something. She is deciding whether to formalise something her team already relies on, which is a materially easier decision and a different sale.
Where the claim breaks. If the security veto fires before adoption spreads, the analyst never gets to advocate. The platform lead is therefore the first actor to satisfy chronologically despite being third by influence, which is the commercial content of the self-hosted decision.
Access map: how a stranger reaches each actor
The analystcold-reachable
Repository, analyst communities, meetups, and the published benchmark. This is the only cold-access path in the whole map. Cost is founder hours
Platform leadno
Reached through documentation before he is reached through conversation. He evaluates artifacts, not pitches
Staff scientistweakly
Through technical publication. Convinced by method and not by demo, and it comes free with the spike
The VPno
Introduced by the analyst after internal use is established, and never prospected. Three to nine months from first install, which is an assumption
Analytics managerno
Through the analyst, or through observing the queue shorten
One conclusion: there is exactly one door. Everything else in this map is reached after that door opens, which is why a single channel runs and why outbound is ruled out. There is no second door to spend money on.
What the roadmap cannot know yet
Whether internal advocacy actually converts, and the three-to-nine-month estimate has nothing behind it. Whether security review fires on the first install or at purchase. Whether accounts hold ten analysts or fifty. And whether the buyer has a line item at all. All four are answerable in the first ten conversations.
