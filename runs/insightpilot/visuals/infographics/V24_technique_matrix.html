The two high-severity gaps

Both concern statistical honesty, not mechanics

Diagnostic questions run contribution analysis across many dimension members with no multiple-comparisons correction, so the system will confidently name noise as a driver and no structural check will catch it. Doubt surfacing has no calibration measurement, so its self-assessment is currently unfalsifiable.

Where the support is oldest

The differentiator rests on imports

Features 15, 16 and 18 draw mostly on audit trails, chain of custody and reproducible research. Disciplines that solved trust-what-you-did-not-do long ago. Technique support that is old and well-tested is a stronger position than novelty.
