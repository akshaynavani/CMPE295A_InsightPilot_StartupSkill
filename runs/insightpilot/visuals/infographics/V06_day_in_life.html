The risk this day surfaced

The beachhead captures the least value of the five

Dr. Chen gets a six-fold reduction. Tom gets fifteen-fold. Marcus gets a category change from guessing to an attributed answer. Angela gains an investigation she could not previously run at all. Priya absorbs the review load the other four shed, and her compensation is the thing she noticed at 17:30 plus a flywheel that has not been observed yet. A champion given work while everyone around them gets leverage does not advocate.

What the day does not show

Four omissions, named

No bad day: no source down, no provider outage, no investigation that runs eleven steps and produces nothing. No silent wrong answer surviving review, which by construction looks exactly like this day. No second analyst recruited, which is the entire premise of the growth loop. And no revenue event, which is correct scope.
