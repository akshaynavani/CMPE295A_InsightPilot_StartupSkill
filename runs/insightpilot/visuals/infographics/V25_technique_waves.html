What this says about novelty

The techniques are old. The composition is not

Forty-three of forty-four foundation techniques are established practice a data engineer would recognise. Any claim of algorithmic novelty at this layer would be overclaiming, and the pack says so in the wave-one summary rather than leaving it implied.
