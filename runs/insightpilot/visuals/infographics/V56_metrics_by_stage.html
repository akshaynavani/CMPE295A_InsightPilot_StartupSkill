Which is why engagement is the wrong frame for this product. A metric that goes up is normally good. Here, driving inspection toward 100% would mean the system had lost their trust entirely. No competitor has an analogous metric, because none has a verify beat to instrument.
The metric this team will most want to report
A working demo. It is the most natural thing to show an advisor and nearly uninformative here, because a demo shows one path working once. Agents above 60% on a single attempt fall below 25% when eight consecutive attempts must all succeed, so a demo that works is consistent with a system that fails three times in four.
The honest substitute is the harness number with its question set published. Less impressive, harder to produce, and the only claim that survives someone re-running it. Second most tempting is reporting accuracy without the silent-error rate, which is the flattering half on its own.
