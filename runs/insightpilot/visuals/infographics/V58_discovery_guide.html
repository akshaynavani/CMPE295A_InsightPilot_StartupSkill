InsightPilot · Discovery guide

Three venture-ending questions are answerable by talking to about fifteen people

The one rule: ask about the past, never about the future. Would you use a tool that does X produces a polite yes which predicts nothing. Walk me through the last time you did X produces a story with times, tools and workarounds in it. The corollary is that the product is never described in a problem interview, because the moment they know what you are building they start being helpful, and helpfulness is noise.

The most consequential hour in the pack
The timed taskSixty minutes, a separate session, run as a measurement and not a demo
Setup
Two matched questions of equal complexity, each spanning a warehouse table and an unmodelled file, on a realistic sample schema. Half the participants take the verify condition first
Verify
They receive a correct answer with hop-level lineage. Task: decide whether you would put your name on this. The stopwatch stops when they commit
Derive
They receive the question and database access. Task: answer this to the point where you would put your name on it
Record
Minutes per condition, which hops they opened and in what order, what they said aloud, accept or reject, and what they did not look at
Measure
Median verify over median derive. Pass at 0.5 or below, fail above 0.8
Do not
Explain the product, defend the design, or answer questions about the interface until the stopwatch stops. Confusion is data
Anti-patterns, named because they are how this gets ruined
1
Pitching in a problem interview. Everything after that is politeness
2
Accepting an estimate where a record exists. Maybe half are multi-source is worthless. Ask them to open the queue and count
3
Interviewing whoever will talk to you. A solo analyst at a thirty-person company has no queue, no buyer and no reviewer. Friendly, and teaches you nothing about this segment
4
Not writing the surprise line. Ten interviews with no surprises were confirmatory, and an empty surprise field is evidence the interviewer was steering
5
Explaining during the timed task. Confusion is the measurement
