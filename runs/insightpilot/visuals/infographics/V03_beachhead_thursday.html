The honest accounting

Nine of the thirty-one minutes were verification

Reading the plan, inspecting step 3, re-running step 5. That is the cost the differentiator has to justify, and it only looks small because the alternative was ninety minutes.

What persists afterward

The correction outlives the question

Her region mapping and her corrected cost definition are written to the organisation's binding store. The next analyst asking a related question inherits both, so the marginal question against this source gets cheaper to verify.

Org-wide  not per-user
Where this journey is fiction

A countable failure is the easy case

An 8% row mismatch is exactly what a cheap structural check finds. The dangerous failures produce a clean-looking join at the wrong grain, with no count anomaly at all. This journey does not show one being caught.
