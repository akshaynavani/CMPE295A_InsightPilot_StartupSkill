Both unsolved items are measurement problems before they are research problems. That is the whole reason the harness is the first thing built and not the twentieth.
The two stack choices worth defending

No agent framework, and DuckDB

A framework that hides the plan in control flow forfeits the approval gate, which is the cheapest error-catch in the system. DuckDB makes a CSV, a Parquet file and a query result the same thing, which is what makes heterogeneous sources tractable for a small team.

What the cost model cannot supply

Expected attempts dominates and is unknown

Verification costs zero model tokens and so does the statistical work, so the frontier surface is narrow. But a roughly one-dollar estimate per investigation assumes one clean pass, and the accuracy figures say retries are the common case.

What a reviewer should conclude

Not a wrapper, and not solved

The verification layer, the lineage model and the cross-source reconciler are real engineering with named methods. The two parts that are not are named on this page rather than buried in an appendix.
