The dangerous combination is G3 passing while G4 fails: cheap verification that does not verify. That ships confident wrong answers wearing an audit trail, which is a worse product than having no trace at all. The gate is written specifically to catch it, which is why both must pass.
Cost to clear all six: about twenty-five founder-hours over four weeks, and no engineering. They can also invalidate the architecture the rest of the semester would implement, which is the uncomfortable part.
