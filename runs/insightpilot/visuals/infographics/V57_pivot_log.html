The pivot this team will be most tempted by
and should refuse

Moving to the business-user market after a disappointing analyst response

If the cost ratio comes back amber and the source-spanning share comes back weak, the instinct will be that the analyst is too sophisticated a customer, and the real market is the person who cannot write the query. That reasoning is superficially strong and it is a closed road, reopened.

1
It is the bet that was already priced. Run with real capital and real distribution, and marked down 73.67%
2
It makes verification unsolvable rather than easier. Removing the analyst does not remove the need to verify. It removes the only person capable of it
3
The evidence for the pivot would be missing. Nothing in the research suggests non-technical self-service works, and the graveyard suggests the opposite four times over

If the analyst market fails, the honest conclusion is that the thesis is wrong, not that a different customer will rescue it.
