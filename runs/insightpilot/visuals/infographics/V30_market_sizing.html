Top-down sanity check

2.2% to 2.8% of the 2026 BI market

Right order of magnitude for an analyst-seat tool in a category whose revenue is dominated by platform licences and warehouse consumption. Had this come out at 20%, the sizing would be wrong.

The uncomfortable conclusion

Not a venture-scale outcome on seats alone

The pack accepts this rather than re-expanding to the BI market. It notes where a larger market would be, since the incumbents already price per question, and declines to size it because no volume data exists to size it with.
