The role this pack has underweighted

The analytics manager has no persona and no journey

He controls the time of the person the whole go-to-market depends on, and his failure mode is silence rather than objection, which makes it invisible until the pilot is already dead. The correction costs an hour: one screening question for analysts about who decides their week, and one manager interview.

The sequencing mistake to avoid

Build the veto holder's artifacts before the first install

Not before the first purchase conversation. His veto is asymmetric, costs him nothing, and fires early in the sequence rather than late.

Four of five do not exist

One of them needs no software at all

The analyst's objection is answerable this month with a stopwatch and five people, and every other row in that table quietly assumes its answer.
