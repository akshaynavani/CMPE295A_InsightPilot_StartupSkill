# Business model canvas

**What this is** — Osterwalder's nine blocks, each carrying **one falsifiable hypothesis and the cheapest test that would kill it**. This is Blank's Customer Discovery Phase 1 artifact.
**Why it exists** — [lean_canvas.md](lean_canvas.md) states what the business *is*; this file states what would have to be true and how little it would cost to find out it is not. The failure it prevents is the standard one: a pack that describes a business model no one ever tries to disprove, so that a wrong assumption is discovered in 295B during the build instead of in 295A over a week of conversations. It also restores the three blocks Maurya's lean canvas drops — Key Partners, Key Activities, Customer Relationships — where two of this venture's live decisions actually sit.
**How to read it** — Read the *Cheapest killing test* column; it is the operational output. A skeptic should attack the Revenue Streams row, which is hypothetical by decision, and Key Resources, where the honest answer is that the team has no proprietary asset.
**Depends on / feeds** — Cross-links to [lean_canvas.md](lean_canvas.md) (which drops blocks 6, 7 and 4 here); inherits personas from [personas.md](personas.md) and pricing from [market_sizing.md](market_sizing.md) §2.3. Feeds `validation/experiment_board.md` directly — every test below should appear there.

## Reading the tests

Three properties of the column above, which are what make it worth having:

1. **Seven of the nine tests cost days, not months.** Rows 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8 are all runnable during 295A. Rows 6 and 9 need a real deployment and are honestly marked as such.
2. **Two tests can each independently invalidate the strategy.** Row 1 kills the neutrality argument and halves the SAM. Row 5 kills the market-type declaration. Neither requires a line of code — both require conversations, which is the cheapest possible way to be wrong.
3. **Row 2 is the venture.** If verification is not cheaper than re-derivation, the differentiator settled in [research/competitors.md](../research/competitors.md) §4 is false, and the product is a slower way to reach an answer the analyst would have written themselves.

## What this canvas concedes

- **Key resources (row 6) is the weak block, and it is weak honestly.** The team holds capability, not assets. The only candidate for a compounding resource cannot be tested before deployment, which is why A3 declines to claim a moat rather than asserting one that sounds good.
- **Revenue streams (row 5) is hypothetical by decision, not omission** (A1). The pack retains the falsifiable price threshold and the cost model, and excludes revenue projection, use of funds and exit comparables — because with no revenue, no CAC and no funnel, any projection would be fabricated.
- **Key partners (row 8) contains a strategic tension.** Consuming dbt and Cube semantic layers is the highest-leverage integration available *and* it deepens dependence on artifacts the customer maintains for someone else's product. Worth doing; worth naming.
